    Our story

    Pehli Kamai is an initiative by Tiny Miracles, a B-Corp certified social enterprise based in Mumbai and Amsterdam. For over a decade, Tiny Miracles has worked alongside communities across the city — on health, on education, on savings, on livelihoods — with one belief holding it all together: people don't lack ability, they lack access.

    Somewhere along the way, a pattern became impossible to ignore. The children we'd watched learn to read had become young adults. They'd finished their 10th, their 12th, some their graduation — often as first-generation learners with no homework or study support, often while working part-time, often without a single person at home who could explain what a resume was or what "HR round" meant, but always with hopes, dreams, and ambition. They'd taught themselves computers on borrowed phones. They'd signed up for Tally courses, animation courses, spoken English classes — on their own, with their own saved money.

    And then they hit a wall that no course could fix: nobody would give them a job.

    So we built the bridge ourselves

    What we have understood is that companies in this city need freshers. Thousands of capable young people need jobs. On paper, that's a simple match. In practice, it isn't — and it's nobody's fault in particular. Hiring is rushed, resumes all look the same, and shortcuts creep in without anyone choosing them.

    A candidate's English, location they are from, clothes, or board of education quietly gets read before their ability does. We've caught ourselves doing it too, and are now unlearning.

    We learnt that where you're from is not a qualification and not a warning sign. An accent is not a skill. A pin code is not a work ethic. Talent was never the shortage in this city — introductions were.

    Why this is a smarter hire for you

    When you set all that bias aside, here's what's left: young people who have solved real problems under real constraints, who understand India's actual mass-market customer better than most campus hires ever will, who are more resourceful and eager to get the job done. None of that fits on a résumé. It shows up on the job.

    Is there sometimes a skills gap? Yes — and that's exactly what our training closes. Candidates on this portal go through career-readiness preparation: reading a job description, building an honest resume, interviewing, and knowing what day one at a workplace actually looks like.

    Who this is for

    Pehli Kamai is an initiative operated by Betterworld Designs Private Limited (CIN U21099MH2020FTC340211), trading as Tiny Miracles, registered office 2nd Floor, Apsara Complex, CJ Plaza, Lamington Road, Grant Road East, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400007. Pehli Kamai introduces young jobseekers ("Youth") in Mumbai to employers ("HR"). This policy explains what we collect from you, why, and what you can do about it, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its 2025 Rules.

    What we collect

    If you create a Youth profile: your name, phone number, email, password, education level, school/college, year of passing, sector and skills, languages, neighbourhood, a short note on the work you're looking for, and — only if you choose to share them — past work experience or volunteering, and a resume file you upload. If you don't upload one, we generate one for you from these details. If you complete our background/work-readiness questionnaire, that assessment and any rating shown on your profile is also collected and shown to employers — if you think a rating is wrong, you can ask us to review it (see Grievance Redressal).

    If you create an HR account: your name, phone, email, password, company name, industry, and city.

    If you use "Let's talk" or "I'm interested": your name, organisation (if any), email, phone (where asked), and your message.

    If you use "Connect via WhatsApp": this opens your own WhatsApp with a pre-filled message to our team containing the candidate's name, sector, and location — sent through WhatsApp (operated by Meta), not through Pehli Kamai's own systems.

    Why we collect it

    • To build and show your profile to prospective employers, or to let you browse candidate profiles and reach out.
    • To generate a resume automatically on your behalf.
    • To notify our team and make an introduction when an employer expresses interest in you, and to stay in touch through your first day on the job and after.
    • To respond to questions sent through our contact forms, and keep a record so we can follow up.
    • With your separate, optional consent only: to feature your story, photo, or outcome in donor updates, impact reports, or social media. You can say no to this and still use the platform fully.

    Where it's stored

    Your account and profile data lives in Google Firebase (Firestore database), hosted in Google Cloud's asia-south1 (Mumbai) region — it stays in India. An uploaded resume file is stored in Firebase Storage. We also keep an internal backup system, operated only by our own team and not shared outside the organisation, as a safeguard against data loss. Email notifications about new signups and enquiries are sent via a transactional email provider. The website itself is hosted on Vercel and GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may operate outside India. None of these providers may use your data for their own purposes — they process it only on our behalf.

    Who sees what

    Anyone visiting the site sees a masked version of each candidate profile — first name and last initial, city-level location only. Full names, exact neighbourhoods, and contact details are visible to any signed-in HR account. Your phone number and email are never shown on your public profile at all; they're only shared with a specific employer once an introduction is made.

    How long we keep it, and what happens after you're placed

    As long as your account is active. You can delete your account and profile yourself, any time, from your account page — this removes it immediately, not after a waiting period. If you'd rather have our team do it for you, contact us (see Grievance Redressal). If you haven't logged in for 24 months, we may reach out before removing an inactive profile, giving at least 48 hours' notice first. Once you're placed in a job, your profile comes down from public browsing; we may keep a record of the placement and any employer feedback for our own reporting, and you can ask to see what's recorded about you.

    Applicants under 18

    You must be 18 or older to create a Youth profile on Pehli Kamai. The sign-up form requires you to confirm this before your profile is created.

    Your rights

    • Access — ask what we hold about you, and who it's been shared with.
    • Correction, completion, and updating — you can edit most of this yourself from your account; contact us for anything you can't.
    • Erasure — ask us to delete your account and data.
    • Withdraw consent — for anything you gave consent for, including the optional donor/impact-report use.
    • Nominate — name someone who can exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapacitated.

    HR users have these same rights over their own account data.

    If something goes wrong

    If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India without delay, followed by a detailed report. There's no minimum severity threshold for this — every breach gets reported, not just the serious ones.

    Pehli Kamai is free for youth

    We will never ask you for money. Creating a profile, being introduced to employers, and using this platform costs you nothing. If anyone claiming to be from Pehli Kamai asks you to pay a fee, a deposit, or hand over original documents, that is not us — tell us immediately.

    Cookies and your browser's local storage

    We don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics on this site. The site does keep a cached copy of your account in your browser's local storage, purely so you're not signed out unexpectedly — this stays on your device.

    Security

    Passwords are held only by Firebase Authentication, hashed and never visible to us. We use established providers for storage and take reasonable steps to protect your data. No online system is completely secure, and we don't claim otherwise.

    Changes to this policy

    If this policy changes in a way that affects how we use your data, we'll post the update here with a new version number and date.

    Keep it accurate

    How an introduction works

    When an employer expresses interest, Pehli Kamai facilitates the introduction. What happens after that — interviews, an offer, the terms of any job — is between the Youth applicant and the employer directly. Pehli Kamai isn't a party to that relationship.

    • We may suspend or revoke your access for breaching these terms, and you agree to indemnify us for any loss arising from your misuse of candidate data.
